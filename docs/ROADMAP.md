# Soma — Roadmap

> What's built, what's next, and why.

This is a living document. Priorities shift based on user feedback and what the data tells us. The ordering reflects current thinking, not a fixed commitment.

---

## Now — v1 (Current Build)

The core loop: log food, log workouts, talk to the AI, discover nearby options.

- [x] AI coach with full meal + workout + goal context
- [x] Meal logging via text description or photo
- [x] Workout logging (manual + Strava sync + Gmail class detection)
- [x] Calorie burn estimation per activity type and duration
- [x] Dashboard with daily snapshot and macro progress
- [x] Ideas tab — nearby restaurants and studios with AI-generated explanations
- [x] Google Maps integration with dark styling and location-aware filtering
- [x] Persistent AI memory across conversations
- [x] Profile, goals, and dietary preferences
- [x] Strava and Gmail OAuth integrations

---

## Next — v1.x (Near-Term Improvements)

Small but high-impact improvements to the existing core loop. No new surfaces.

**AI Coach**
- Multi-turn tool use: log a meal or workout directly from the chat ("log that as breakfast")
- Smarter context window: weight recent data more than older entries
- Confidence indicators on macro estimates ("low confidence — restaurant meal with no menu")

**Meal Logging**
- Quick re-log: surface recently eaten meals for one-tap re-entry
- Meal templates: save a custom meal for repeated logging
- Micronutrient estimates (sodium, sugar, saturated fat) for users who track beyond macros

**Workout Tracking**
- Exercise-level detail: log sets, reps, and weight within a strength session
- Weekly training load summary (total volume, intensity distribution)
- Rest day detection and recovery nudge

**Ideas Tab**
- Save a place to favourites
- Filter by distance radius (500m / 1km / 2km)
- Refresh results without re-granting location

## Next — v2 (New Surfaces)

Larger bets that require new infrastructure or new user mental models. Sequenced after v1.x is stable.

**Oura Ring Integration**
Connect resting heart rate, HRV, and sleep score to the AI coach context. The coach can factor in recovery quality when suggesting training intensity or calorie targets. This is the single highest-leverage integration for the target user.

**Adaptive Goal Recalibration**
Goals that update automatically based on trends. If a user consistently trains 5 days/week but their target is 4, the goal recalibrates. If calorie intake is consistently 200kcal below target with no apparent weight change, the system flags a possible TDEE miscalculation.

**Proactive Coach Nudges**
The AI initiates check-ins when patterns suggest something worth surfacing — three days without a workout logged, protein consistently below target, a big training week followed by poor sleep. Not push notifications; more like a morning briefing card on the dashboard.

**Weekly Review**
A Sunday summary: how the week went across nutrition, training, and recovery. What went well, what to adjust, what to focus on next week. Generated by the AI coach from the user's full data.

**Progress Photos**
Body composition tracking over time. Photo upload with date stamp, optional AI-assisted trend observation (not body analysis — just visual delta over time).

## Later — v3 (Scale & Platform)

Ideas that require significant investment or depend on v2 features being proven out first.

**Meal Plan Generation**
Weekly meal scaffolding based on macro targets, dietary preferences, and what's typically available near the user. The gap between "here are your targets" and "here's what to eat" is still manual for most users.

**Native Mobile Apps**
A React Native or Swift/Kotlin app for better camera access, background sync, widget support, and proper push notifications. The web app covers the core use case, but native unlocks the full platform.

**Coaching Plans**
Structured programmes (e.g. "8-week strength block", "marathon prep") that the AI monitors and adjusts based on actual performance. Bridges the gap between self-directed tracking and coached training.

**Team / Household Mode**
Shared goals and visibility for couples or small groups who train and eat together. A secondary use case, but a strong referral driver.

---

## What We're Not Building

Some things are explicitly out of scope, even if users ask for them:

- **Calorie counting gamification** (streaks, badges, leaderboards) — this attracts the wrong user and creates the wrong relationship with food
- **Social feed or community** — adds moderation overhead without improving the core product
- **Supplement tracking or recommendations** — outside our expertise and trust model
- **Medical or clinical features** — Soma is a personal tool, not a healthcare product
